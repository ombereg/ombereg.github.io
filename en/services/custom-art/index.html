Can you create artwork from a personal story?

Yes. A few words, a memory or meaningful symbols can be the starting point.

Is this a physical painting or a digital file?

The basic commission is a digital artwork. Printing, materials and delivery are separate arrangements if available.

Can I send reference photographs?

Yes, provided you have permission to use the images and any necessary likeness rights.
